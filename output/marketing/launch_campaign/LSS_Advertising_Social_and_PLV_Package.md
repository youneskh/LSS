## Organic social copy

### Post 1 - launch

**Copy:** A batch is more than a production order. It is the material, steps, checks, decisions, and people behind a product.

When that story lives across separate systems, spreadsheets, and inboxes, every investigation starts with reconstruction.

LSS - Life Sciences Suite is designed to connect enterprise operations and quality processes into one traceable digital environment for life-sciences organisations.

Software does not guarantee compliance. It can, however, support the controlled workflows, records, and evidence your organisation configures and validates for its intended use.

**CTA:** Which workflow is hardest for your team to trace?  
**Visual:** `social/lss_social_batch_story.svg` - 1080 x 1080 px.

### Post 2 - educational

**Copy:** Before you ask whether a system has “traceability,” ask five more useful questions:

1. Can we start from a questioned lot?
2. Can we see relevant upstream and downstream context?
3. Can we identify the records and decisions that changed its status?
4. Can the right role access that context without manual reconstruction?
5. Can we demonstrate the configured workflow for our intended use?

Traceability is not a dashboard claim. It is a workflow design question.

**CTA:** Save this checklist for your next process review.

### Post 3 - thought leadership

**Copy:** ERP records explain transactions. Quality records explain decisions. Regulated teams need both in context.

The most productive digitalisation projects do not begin with an app list. They begin with one decision that is difficult to explain today - then map the records, roles, exceptions, and evidence behind it.

**CTA:** Request a workflow discovery.

### Production specification

Export print artwork as PDF/X-4 with 3 mm bleed and crop marks. Keep text at least 15 mm inside final trim for poster/roll-up. Use CMYK conversion supplied by the printer; retain editable SVG masters. All screen/screenshot claims need product-owner sign-off. The footer disclaimer is required on PLV smaller than A3 and on all paid social landing pages.
