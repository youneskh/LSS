## 0. Truth protocol and launch guardrails

**Fact (source-reviewed).** LSS is a modular life-sciences platform built on Odoo 19 Community Edition, PostgreSQL, and custom life-sciences modules. Its intended architecture spans ERP, a common QMS platform, and industry extensions for pharmaceutical, medical-device, cosmetics, medical-plastics, and laboratory operations.

**Capability wording.** Use “designed to support,” “provides controls that can support,” and “can be configured and validated according to applicable requirements.”

**Never publish without verification:** certification/compliance claims; electronic-signature, recall, serialization, calibration, analytics, complaint, supplier-quality, and validation feature detail; implementation duration; integrations; customer outcomes; customer names; regulatory/legal assertions. The implementation guides identify several specialised functions as custom development and some Community assumptions as needing correction. Therefore, all feature-specific campaign copy must pass a product-readiness review.

**Mandatory disclaimer:** “LSS is designed to support regulated workflows. Compliance depends on the organisation’s processes, configuration, validation, training, and ongoing quality system; LSS does not itself certify compliance.”

## I. Brand strategy

### Positioning, promise and identity

**Positioning statement.** For regulated life-sciences organisations that need operations and quality to work as one, LSS is a modular business platform that connects ERP, manufacturing, quality processes, controlled information, and traceability in one accountable digital environment. Built on an Odoo 19 Community foundation, LSS is shaped around life-sciences workflows rather than marketed as a generic ERP.

**Brand promise.** Make every critical operational decision easier to trace, explain, and improve.

**Mission.** Help regulated organisations replace disconnected operational and quality work with purposeful digital workflows and usable evidence.

**Vision.** A life-sciences operating environment where product, batch, quality, and decision history are connected by design.

**Values.** Evidence before assertion; precision over hype; accountability by design; practical adoption; modular progress; respect for quality professionals.

**Personality and voice.** Assured, technically literate, calm, exact, collaborative, and candid about boundaries. Short declarative sentences. Lead with the operational consequence, then the workflow, then the capability. Say “trace a batch from receipt through disposition” rather than “unlock visibility.”

**Messaging principles.**

1. Start with a regulated workflow, not a feature list.
2. Separate product capability, compliance outcome, and customer validation.
3. Name a user and a decision whenever possible.
4. Make Odoo the technology foundation, never the lead commercial identity.
5. Use proof only when source-controlled; otherwise label `[REQUIRES PRODUCT VERIFICATION]`.

**Differentiation.** LSS brings ERP and quality-process design into the same modular architecture, allowing the commercial conversation to begin with a customer’s workflow - material receipt, batch record, investigation, document approval, or release - rather than a disconnected app catalogue.

**Competitive position.** Against generic ERP: life-sciences workflow focus. Against stand-alone QMS: operational context. Against heavily customised legacy stacks: modular architecture and a common data environment. Do not claim superior cost, speed, or breadth without evidence.

### Pitches and taglines

**Elevator pitch.** LSS connects enterprise operations and quality processes into one traceable digital environment designed for life-sciences organisations.

**30 seconds.** Regulated manufacturers often manage production, quality, documents, and traceability in separate systems and spreadsheets. LSS brings these workflows into a connected platform: ERP and manufacturing at the operational core, with life-sciences capabilities around quality, controlled records, and traceability. It is designed to support the way regulated teams investigate, review, release, and improve - subject to each organisation’s own configuration and validation programme.

**60 seconds.** A batch should not become hard to explain because information sits in purchasing, a spreadsheet, a QMS, and an inbox. LSS is a life-sciences business platform built on an Odoo 19 Community ERP foundation and extended with specialised modules. It is designed to connect materials, production, quality events, documents, and downstream decisions. The result is a more coherent operating environment for teams responsible for manufacturing, quality, supply chain, and IT. LSS supports regulated workflows; it does not replace the customer’s quality system or validation responsibilities.

**2-minute explanation.** LSS addresses a recurring problem in regulated operations: transactions move through ERP, quality events live elsewhere, documents circulate by email, and investigating a batch means reconstructing a story after the fact. LSS is designed as one modular environment. Its ERP foundation supports purchasing, inventory, manufacturing, sales, accounting, and maintenance. A life-sciences layer is intended to add controlled quality workflows such as documents, deviations, CAPA, training, calibration, supplier qualification, complaints, recalls, and validation evidence; industry extensions address the particular workflow needs of pharmaceutical, device, cosmetic, plastics, and laboratory teams. The commercial value is not “compliance in a box.” It is better-connected evidence, decisions, and accountability. During discovery, LSS maps the customer’s actual processes, confirms available modules and integrations, and defines the configuration, controls, and validation work needed for the intended use.

**Taglines.** “Traceability that connects operations and quality.” / “One connected environment for regulated operations.” / “See the complete journey of a batch.” / “Operational control. Quality context. Traceable decisions.”

**Recommendation:** **“Traceability that connects operations and quality.”** Supporting slogans: “From material receipt to batch decision.” “Designed for the work behind regulated products.” “Bring the batch story together.”

**Messaging hierarchy.** Master message -> connected operational and quality environment -> traceable workflows -> modular life-sciences architecture -> verified, industry-specific capabilities -> request a workflow discovery/demo.

**Approved core claims.** “Designed for regulated life-sciences workflows.” “Connects operational and quality context.” “Modular architecture on an Odoo 19 Community foundation.” “Supports traceability-oriented process design.”

**Claims prohibited.** FDA-approved; GMP/ISO/ANPP-certified; 100% compliant; guaranteed audit readiness; native Odoo quality/documents/sign claims; verified customer outcomes without source data; “only” or “world-leading.”

### Visual identity direction

**System:** deep navy `#102A43`, clinical teal `#0E7C86`, steel `#5C6B73`, off-white `#F7FAFC`, signal amber `#D98E04` for alerts only. Use a geometric sans serif (Inter/IBM Plex Sans) plus restrained mono labels for IDs and workflow states. Photography: clean production, lab, controlled warehouse, real people at work; avoid generic “scientist holding tablet.” Diagrams use a single left-to-right flow, trace lines, record identifiers, and clear ownership. Generous whitespace, thin dividers, evidence-card layouts. Avoid gradients, neon, glassmorphism, stock-startup illustrations, and oversized claims.
