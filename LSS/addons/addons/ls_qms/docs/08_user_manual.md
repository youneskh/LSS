# 08 — User Manual

## 1. Writing and issuing a procedure

1. Quality Management, Procedures, New.
2. Fill the title, the department, the purpose, the scope and the body. The
   reference is allocated automatically on saving.
3. Add reviewers if the document must be checked by named persons. Each of
   them receives an activity when the document is submitted.
4. Set the review period in months. Zero disables the periodic review.
5. **Submit for Review**. The document becomes read only for its content.
6. The approver opens the document and clicks **Approve**, or **Reject** and
   states a reason. A rejection returns the document to draft.
7. The approver sets the effective date, then clicks **Publish**. Left empty,
   the effective date is the current date.

The author cannot approve their own document while the segregation parameter
is active.

## 2. Revising a published document

1. Open the published revision and click **New Revision**.
2. State the reason for change. It is mandatory and is stored on the new
   revision.
3. The new revision opens in draft with version incremented by one and the
   same reference. The previous revision moves to the state under revision.
4. Follow the issuing path of section 1. When the new revision is published,
   the previous one becomes obsolete automatically and its withdrawal date is
   stamped.

## 3. Reading the review status

| Indicator | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Up to date | The review date is beyond the reminder window |
| Due soon | The review date falls within the reminder window |
| Overdue | The review date has passed |
| Not applicable | The review period is zero, or the document is not published |

The list **Reporting, Procedures Due for Review** is filtered on the two
states that require attention.

## 4. Managing a quality objective

1. Quality Management, Quality Objectives, New.
2. Fill the title, the responsible person, the start date and the target date.
3. Choose the direction: increase, decrease, or maintain.
4. Fill the baseline and the target. For a maintain objective, fill the
   tolerance as well.
5. **Start**. The objective moves to in progress.
6. Add a measurement in the tab Measurements at each period. The current
   value, the achievement rate and the performance status recompute.
7. Close the objective with **Achieved** or **Not Achieved**.

Achievement is capped at nothing above one hundred percent: an objective
exceeded shows a rate above one hundred, which is intentional.

## 5. Recording evidence

1. Quality Management, Quality Records, New.
2. Choose the type, the date, and fill the description and the conclusion.
3. Link the record to the policy, the procedure, the quality plan or the
   objective it relates to.
4. Set the retention period. Zero means an unlimited retention.
5. **Confirm**. The record becomes read only for a quality user. Only a
   quality manager can correct it afterwards, and the correction is tracked in
   the chatter.
6. **Archive** when the record leaves active use.
7. **Dispose** is available to the manager only, on an archived record whose
   retention date has passed.

## 6. Printing

The Print menu of a policy, a procedure or a quality plan produces a PDF
carrying the control block and the notice stating that the printed copy is
not controlled. The filename is the reference followed by the version.
