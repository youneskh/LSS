Life Sciences — Cosmetics

Formulations, safety reports, Product Information Files, claim substantiation and labelling for Regulation (EC) No 1223/2009.

Odoo 19.0 Community Edition · AGPL-3.0 · 13 models · 316 fields

What this module does not do

It supports the implementation of processes required by Regulation (EC) No 1223/2009. It does not certify compliance, and installing it does not make an organisation compliant.

It ships no annex substance data, because Annexes II–VI are amended several times a year and an embedded snapshot would be stale and falsely authoritative. Until you link an ingredient to an annex entry you have loaded yourself, the module reports its status as not evaluated — never as compliant.

The regulation, mapped to fields

Provision How it is enforced
Annex I
Safety report		 Part A §1–10 and Part B §1–4 reproduced as fields. Part B cannot start until all ten Part A sections are documented, because Part B §3 requires the reasoning to rest on them.
Art. 10(2)
Assessor qualification		 Name, address and qualification evidence recorded; approval refused for any user other than the account named in section B4. The module records the evidence — it cannot verify a diploma.
Art. 11(1)
Ten-year retention		 Retention end date computed from the last batch placed on the market. Archiving before it is refused; deletion outside draft is blocked.
Art. 11(2)
Five file items		 One page each. Activation names whatever is missing, and refuses an unapproved safety report, a "not safe" conclusion, or an unapproved claim.
Art. 19(1)
Seven particulars		 One field per particular. The ingredient list is generated from the approved formulation, applying descending weight order, the (nano) suffix, parfum, colorant ordering and the +/- marker. The 30-month durability rule is enforced.
EU 655/2013
Claim criteria		 Six criteria, each with a tick and a written justification. A tick alone will not approve a claim.
Algeria
Prior authorisation		 The sixteen dossier items, the 45-day decision period and the 1-month compliance period, per décret exécutif n° 97-37 as modified by n° 10-114. Cosmetics fall under the Ministère du Commerce — no ANPP requirement is asserted.

Data integrity

  • Approved formulations, safety reports and labels are frozen — changes go through a new version, which supersedes its predecessor only when itself approved.
  • Segregation of duties is enforced at ORM level: the submitter cannot approve, and the safety report can only be signed by the named assessor.
  • Scheduled actions post notices, never decide. No cron archives a dossier or withdraws an authorisation.
  • Absence of reference data is reported as not evaluated, never as compliant.
