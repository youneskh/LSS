Validation Management

Master plans, IQ/OQ/PQ protocols, execution records, discrepancies and validation summary reports

This module manages the validation lifecycle of equipment, utilities, processes, cleaning procedures, computerised systems and analytical methods in regulated life sciences environments.

  • Controlled Validation Master Plans with versioning.
  • Protocols whose test cases and acceptance criteria are frozen at approval.
  • Execution records with per test attribution, discrepancy handling and segregation between executor and reviewer.
  • Validation Summary Reports granting a dated validated status to the item.
  • Append-only electronic signature log with a SHA-256 hash chain.
  • Daily scheduled action refreshing statuses and raising revalidation activities.

The module is designed to support the implementation of processes aligned with GMP, ISO 9001, ISO 13485, EU GMP Annex 15 and FDA 21 CFR Part 11. It does not certify compliance: validation, procedures and training remain the responsibility of the organisation.
