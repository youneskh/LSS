Life Sciences – Audit Management

Plan, conduct, report and close internal and supplier audits inside Odoo 19 Community.

This module manages the full audit lifecycle for regulated manufacturing: the annual audit programme, the auditor qualification register, versioned checklist templates, the fieldwork record, the findings raised from it, the auditee responses, the effectiveness verification and the issued audit report.

What it enforces

  • Impartiality. An audit cannot be saved when a member of the team is also an auditee, or owns one of the audited areas.
  • Auditor qualification. An audit cannot be scheduled with an unqualified lead auditor, an expired qualification, or an auditor working outside their qualified scope.
  • Segregation of duties on reports. The person who prepares a report can neither review nor approve it.
  • Evidence. A question cannot be marked non-conform or observation without recorded evidence.
  • Verification before closure. A finding cannot be closed without a documented verification method and result, and never by the auditee who answered it.
  • Record immutability. Closed audits, closed findings and issued reports become read-only; approved checklists cannot be edited.

Traceability by design

Checklist question text is copied into the audit when the checklist is loaded. Publishing a later template version therefore never alters what a past audit actually asked. Checklists are versioned and approved; the version used is recorded on the audit.

Programmes, audits, findings and reports each carry a sequence-issued reference (APG, AUD, FND, ARP). Status changes, ownership and dates are tracked in the chatter of every record.

Important notice on regulatory compliance

This module is designed to support the implementation of audit processes. It does not certify compliance with any framework, and it is not validated software. Compliance depends on the organisation's procedures, its computer system validation, staff training and quality system.

The approvals in this module are not 21 CFR Part 11 electronic signatures. They record the acting user, a timestamp and a tracked change history. They do not implement the signature manifestation and re-authentication requirements of 21 CFR 11.50 and 11.200. Sites needing Part 11 signatures must add a dedicated electronic signature component.
