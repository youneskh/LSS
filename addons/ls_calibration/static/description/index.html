Calibration Management

Life Sciences Suite for Odoo 19 Community

Instrument register, calibration plans, calibration records with as-found and as-left readings, out-of-tolerance handling and calibration certificates, for pharmaceutical manufacturing, medical devices, medical plastics, cosmetics and quality control laboratories.

What it does

  • Register every measuring, monitoring and test instrument with its range, unit, maximum permissible error, criticality and GxP impact.
  • Define a calibration plan per instrument: interval, written procedure reference and test points with their acceptance limits.
  • Follow the calibration status of the whole site: valid, due soon, overdue, not scheduled or not applicable.
  • Record the as-found readings before any adjustment and the as-left readings after it. The verdict of each point and the overall result are computed.
  • Issue calibration certificates, or register those received from an external accredited laboratory.
  • Print the calibration record and the certificate.

Four controls enforced by the server

As-found before adjustment

An out-of-tolerance as-found result cannot be submitted without a documented assessment of its impact on the products already released.

Metrological traceability

A reference standard whose own calibration is overdue is refused.

Segregation of duties

The user who performed a calibration cannot approve it, whatever the interface used.

Immutable evidence

An approved record and its test points can no longer be modified or deleted, through the interface or through the external API.

Regulatory statement

This module supports the implementation of processes aligned with the control-of-measuring-equipment requirements of ISO 9001:2015, ISO 13485:2016 and the FDA Quality Management System Regulation. It does not establish compliance with any framework and is not certified against any of them. It does not provide FDA 21 CFR Part 11 electronic signatures: the approval records the signer, the time and the meaning of the signature, but does not re-authenticate the signer.
