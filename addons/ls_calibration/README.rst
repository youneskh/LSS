======================================
Life Sciences - Calibration Management
======================================

This module manages the calibration of the measuring, monitoring and test
instruments used in regulated Life Sciences environments: pharmaceutical
manufacturing, medical devices, medical plastics, cosmetics and quality
control laboratories.

It covers the instrument register, the periodic calibration plans and their
test points, the calibration records carrying the as-found and as-left
readings, the handling of out-of-tolerance situations, the review and
approval of the results with segregation of duties, and the calibration
certificates issued internally or received from an external laboratory.
