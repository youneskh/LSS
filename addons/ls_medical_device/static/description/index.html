Life Sciences Suite — Medical Devices

Regulatory record management for Odoo 19 Community

Device register, UDI assignments, ISO 14971 risk management, clinical evaluation and post-market clinical follow-up, technical documentation, CE marking, and post-market surveillance with periodic reporting.

What it manages

  • Device register — a gated six-state life cycle from draft to withdrawal, with justified status changes.
  • UDI assignments — Basic UDI-DI, UDI-DI and UDI-PI components across packaging levels.
  • Risk management — the ISO 14971:2019 chain from hazard to residual risk evaluation.
  • Clinical evaluation — plan, evidence, appraisal, analysis and benefit-risk conclusion.
  • Technical documentation — section index with completeness control and evidence references.
  • CE marking — conformity route, notified body certificate, declaration of conformity, automatic expiry.
  • Post-market surveillance — plans and periodic reports under MDR Articles 85 and 86.
  • PMCF — periodic evaluation reports with the annual update obligation for class III.

Segregation of duties

Four access levels: Viewer, User, Regulatory Affairs and Manager. Approval authority is enforced inside the model methods, not only in the views, so it also applies to imports and integrations. An approver may not approve a record they authored. Approved records are cancelled rather than deleted.

What it does not do

This module does not certify compliance with any regulatory framework. It records and structures information; conformity is established by the organisation through its quality system, its validation activities and, where required, a notified body.

Vigilance case management under MDR Article 87, EUDAMED submission, electronic signatures and the Annex VIII classification rules are outside its scope. Risk scales, matrix boundaries and documentation section titles ship as editable configuration that must be reviewed before use.
