Life Sciences - Complaint Management

Receive, assess, investigate, resolve and close product quality complaints

This module adds a complaint handling process designed for regulated life sciences organisations: pharmaceutical manufacturing, medical devices, medical plastics, cosmetics and laboratories.

  • Complaint intake with complainant, product and lot traceability
  • Configurable categories, severities and target dates
  • Root cause investigation with segregation of duties on approval
  • Adverse event records with a configurable reporting deadline
  • Resolution actions with completion evidence
  • Closure review, record freezing and printable complaint record
  • Four access levels: viewer, investigator, reviewer, manager

No regulatory timeline is pre-configured. Every target and deadline must be set by the deploying organisation on the complaint category, on the basis of the regulations that apply to it.
