# Phase 1 — Business Analysis

## 1. Business objectives

| # | Objective |
|---|---|
| BO-01 | Register every complaint received about a marketed product or a delivered service, from any reception channel, in a single system of record. |
| BO-02 | Classify each complaint by category, type and severity so that handling effort is proportionate to risk. |
| BO-03 | Identify, at intake, complaints that carry a potential safety or regulatory consequence, and route them to a vigilance sub-process. |
| BO-04 | Establish the root cause of confirmed complaints and record the evidence supporting the conclusion. |
| BO-05 | Record the actions taken towards the complainant and towards the product, with objective evidence of completion. |
| BO-06 | Close each complaint only after an independent review, and freeze the record afterwards. |
| BO-07 | Make complaint data analysable over time (by product, lot, category, root cause, market) to support trending. |
| BO-08 | Provide a printable complaint record suitable for inclusion in an inspection or audit file. |

## 2. Business requirements

| # | Requirement | Objective |
|---|---|---|
| BR-01 | Every complaint carries a unique, non-reusable reference generated by the system. | BO-01 |
| BR-02 | Reception channel, receipt date and receiving user are mandatory at creation. | BO-01 |
| BR-03 | A product related complaint identifies the product, and the lot either as a system record or as free text when the lot is unknown to the system. | BO-01, BO-07 |
| BR-04 | The complaint category drives the target dates; the targets are configurable and may be left unset. | BO-02 |
| BR-05 | An assessment is documented before any investigation starts. | BO-02, BO-03 |
| BR-06 | Adverse events are recorded as separate records with their own lifecycle and their own reportability decision. | BO-03 |
| BR-07 | A reportability decision is never recorded without a written rationale. | BO-03 |
| BR-08 | An investigation cannot be approved by the person who performed it. | BO-04, BO-06 |
| BR-09 | A root cause that is not "not determined" requires a description. | BO-04 |
| BR-10 | A resolution cannot be marked done without completion evidence. | BO-05 |
| BR-11 | A complaint cannot be closed while a resolution or an adverse event is open, or while a declared CAPA has no reference. | BO-06 |
| BR-12 | The closure reviewer differs from the responsible user. | BO-06 |
| BR-13 | Closed and cancelled complaints are read-only; correction happens by raising a new complaint. | BO-06 |
| BR-14 | Deletion is impossible once a record left its initial state. | BO-06 |
| BR-15 | Cancellation is possible but requires a documented reason. | BO-06 |
| BR-16 | Complaints, investigations, adverse events and resolutions are groupable and analysable in list, graph and pivot views. | BO-07 |
| BR-17 | A complete complaint record can be printed as a PDF. | BO-08 |
| BR-18 | Overdue complaints and overdue adverse event reports produce a notification to the responsible user. | BO-02, BO-03 |

## 3. Stakeholders

| Stakeholder | Interest |
|---|---|
| Quality Assurance | Owns the process, approves investigations, closes complaints. |
| Regulatory Affairs / Vigilance | Owns reportability decisions and submissions to authorities; configures deadlines. |
| Customer Service / Commercial | Receives complaints and communicates outcomes to complainants. |
| Manufacturing and Packaging | Provides batch records and process evidence during investigations. |
| Quality Control laboratory | Examines returned samples. |
| Management | Monitors volumes, ageing and recurrence. |
| Internal and external auditors, inspectors | Read the records and the audit history. |
| Complainant (patient, healthcare professional, pharmacy, distributor) | Expects acknowledgement and an outcome. |

## 4. User roles

| Role | Group | Capabilities |
|---|---|---|
| Complaint Viewer | `group_ls_complaint_viewer` | Read complaints, investigations, resolutions, adverse events and categories. No write. |
| Complaint Investigator | `group_ls_complaint_investigator` | Create complaints; write the complaints he owns or received; create and conduct investigations, resolutions and adverse events. Cannot approve, close or delete. |
| Complaint Reviewer | `group_ls_complaint_reviewer` | Everything an investigator can do, on any complaint; approve or reject investigations; waive investigations; close complaints. Cannot delete or configure. |
| Complaint Manager | `group_ls_complaint_manager` | Everything a reviewer can do; cancel and reset complaints; delete records; configure categories. |

Groups are cumulative: manager implies reviewer implies investigator implies
viewer. Segregation of duties is enforced per record (an approver cannot be the
investigator of that record; a closure reviewer cannot be the responsible user
of that complaint), not by making the groups mutually exclusive.

## 5. User stories

| # | Story |
|---|---|
| US-01 | As a customer service agent, I register a complaint received by phone in under two minutes, so that the receipt date is accurate. |
| US-02 | As a quality assurance officer, I see the complaints that passed their closure target, so that I can act on the oldest first. |
| US-03 | As a quality assurance officer, I classify a complaint and the system proposes the severity configured for that category. |
| US-04 | As an investigator, I record a 5-Whys analysis and the resulting root cause, so that the conclusion is traceable. |
| US-05 | As a quality manager, I approve an investigation I did not perform, so that the independence of the review is evidenced. |
| US-06 | As a vigilance officer, I record an adverse event, decide its reportability with a written rationale, and record the submission reference. |
| US-07 | As a vigilance officer, I am notified when a reportable event reaches its configured deadline. |
| US-08 | As a quality manager, I close a complaint only after confirming that the resolution was effective. |
| US-09 | As an auditor, I print the complete complaint record with its investigations, adverse events and resolutions. |
| US-10 | As a quality manager, I analyse complaints per product and per root cause over a year, so that recurrence is visible. |
| US-11 | As a quality manager, I cancel a duplicate complaint with a reason instead of deleting it. |
| US-12 | As a system administrator, I define the categories and the target days that my own procedure requires. |

## 6. Use cases

| # | Use case | Primary actor | Main flow |
|---|---|---|---|
| UC-01 | Register a complaint | Investigator | Enter complainant, product, lot, description → save → reference assigned, status Received. |

| UC-03 | Investigate | Investigator | *Start Investigation* → a draft investigation is created → start, document, complete. |
| UC-04 | Approve an investigation | Reviewer | Open the completed investigation → *Approve*. Refused if the approver is the investigator. |
| UC-05 | Waive an investigation | Reviewer | Only when the category does not require one and no adverse event exists → justification is mandatory. |
| UC-06 | Declare a CAPA | Reviewer | *CAPA Required* with justification → record the external CAPA reference → *Start Resolution*. |
| UC-07 | Record a resolution | Investigator | Create the resolution, start it, record evidence, mark done. |
| UC-08 | Record an adverse event | Investigator | Create the event, document seriousness, outcome and causality, decide reportability with rationale, *Assess*. |
| UC-09 | Submit an adverse event report | Investigator | *Record Submission* with the authority acknowledgement reference. |
| UC-10 | Close a complaint | Reviewer | *Close* → wizard: reviewer, closure summary, effectiveness confirmation → record frozen. |
| UC-11 | Cancel a complaint | Manager | *Cancel* → wizard: reason → record frozen, reversible by *Reset to Received*. |
| UC-12 | Print the record | Any reader | Print → *Complaint Record*. |
| UC-13 | Analyse complaints | Any reader | Reporting → Complaint Analysis → pivot and graph. |
| UC-14 | Configure categories | Manager | Configuration → Complaint Categories. |

## 7. Functional scope

In scope: complaint intake, classification, assessment, investigation with root
cause, adverse event recording and reporting follow-up, resolution actions,
closure review, cancellation, freezing of final records, target date derivation,
overdue notification, printable record, list/graph/pivot analysis, four access
levels, multi-company isolation, two mail templates for complainant
communication.

## 8. Out of scope

Explicitly not delivered by this module:

1. CAPA record management — only a reference field and a justification are held.
2. Recall and field safety corrective action execution — only a resolution type exists.
3. Electronic signatures meeting FDA 21 CFR Part 11 — see `docs/02_regulatory_analysis.md`.
4. A dedicated GxP audit trail beyond Odoo field tracking — see the same document.
5. Document management and controlled attachments beyond Odoo attachments.
6. Automated transmission of reports to any authority portal or gateway.
7. Trending statistics with automatic signal detection.
8. Integration with laboratory (LIMS) or manufacturing (MES) systems.
9. Customer portal access for complainants.
10. Translations: no `.po` file is delivered, only the source strings.

## 9. Risks

| # | Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| RI-01 | The module is not installable on Odoo 19 because of an unverified API. | High | Risk register with fallbacks in `docs/00_verification_and_limitations.md`; legacy-API checker executed. |
| RI-02 | Users interpret "supports ISO 13485" as "is compliant". | High | Every regulatory statement is phrased as *supports implementation of*; the limitation document states the opposite explicitly. |
| RI-03 | An organisation relies on a default reporting deadline that does not apply to it. | High | No deadline is delivered; the field defaults to *not configured*. |
| RI-04 | Field tracking is mistaken for a compliant audit trail. | High | Stated in the regulatory analysis and in the administrator manual. |
| RI-05 | The ownership record rule blocks legitimate work in small teams. | Medium | Documented in the administrator manual with the procedure to relax it. |
| RI-06 | Personal data of patients is entered in the adverse event record. | High | Only pseudonym, age range and sex fields exist; a warning is displayed on the form. |
| RI-07 | The absence of a CAPA module leaves the CAPA reference unverified. | Medium | The reference is mandatory before resolution when a CAPA was declared; a bridge module is specified in the developer manual. |
| RI-08 | Freezing closed records frustrates users who expected to correct them. | Low | Documented behaviour; correction is by new complaint, which is the regulated practice. |
